Palestine in 10 minutes

For first-time visitors: ten topics, one minute each, with the key figures – and from each topic the way to the detailed page with all sources (in German).

About this page. This website is in German; this page is an English summary of its core findings. Each section takes about a minute to read, gives the key figures and links to the German page where everything is set out in detail with sources and dates. Figures are rounded here; their sources and cut-off dates are on the linked pages. Deutsche Fassung · Versión en español

Minute 1

A land with people – for millennia

Palestine was never empty. According to ancient DNA, most of the people who live there descend from the population that lived in the land in the Bronze Age. Rulers changed over 4,000 years, the people stayed; the Arab conquest brought mainly language and religion. Until 1948 the villages still bore the ancient place names. “A land without a people” was a political slogan, not a description.

757,182inhabitants in the 1922 British census, about 11 % of them Jews
4,000years of continuous settlement, shown by ancient DNA, place names and language

Read more (in German): Origins of the Palestinians (English) · Eras · “Land without a people” checked (English)

Minute 2

Zionism and the Mandate: a state for some, silence about the others

In the late 19th century Zionism emerged in Europe as a response to antisemitism: a Jewish state in Palestine. In 1917 Britain promised a “national home” – the Balfour Declaration mentioned nine tenths of the population only as “existing non-Jewish communities”. Under British rule Jewish immigration grew, Palestinians demanded independence, and there were uprisings on both sides.

1917Balfour Declaration
1922–1948British Mandate

Read more (in German): Zionism & statehood · Basel 1897 · Jews in the land

Minute 3

1948: the Nakba

With the founding of Israel and the war of 1947–1949, 700,000 to 750,000 Palestinians were expelled or fled – and were never allowed to return, although the UN demanded it in 1948 (Resolution 194). Some 418 villages were depopulated and mostly destroyed, their land expropriated. The refugees and their descendants still live in camps in Gaza, the West Bank, Lebanon, Syria and Jordan.

700,000–750,000expelled or fled 1947–1949
418depopulated villages (Walid Khalidi)
5.9 mrefugees registered with UNRWA today

Read more (in German): The Nakba 1948 (English) · Chronicle of massacres · The refugee camps

Minute 4

1967 to today: occupation and settlements

Since 1967 Israel has occupied the West Bank including East Jerusalem, and Gaza. In 2024 the International Court of Justice found this occupation unlawful and ruled that it must end as rapidly as possible. Yet the settlements keep growing – in 2025 more new ones were approved than ever before, and the government openly pursues annexation. Palestinians live under military law, settlers under Israeli civil law.

over 750,000settlers in the West Bank and East Jerusalem
925checkpoints, gates and roadblocks (OCHA, Dec. 2025)
2024ICJ: the occupation is unlawful

Read more (in German): West Bank & East Jerusalem (English) · Annexation · Settler violence · Law in daily life

Minute 5

Gaza: siege, attacks – and hunger as a weapon

Gaza – 365 square kilometres, about two million people – has been under blockade since 2007. After the Hamas attack of 7 October 2023, with about 1,200 killed in Israel and 251 hostages, Israel launched an assault that largely destroyed the Gaza Strip. About 90 % of the population were displaced; according to UN findings, starvation was used as a weapon; schools, hospitals and universities lie in ruins. People are still being killed since the ceasefire of October 2025 – according to the UN Commission, the genocide continues.

74,618killed in Gaza since 7 Oct 2023 (Ministry of Health via OCHA, 4 Nov 2026)
1,613killed since the ceasefire began (OCHA, 9 Nov 2026)
191Palestinian media workers killed (CPJ)

Read more (in German): Gaza (English) · Displacement in Gaza · Education, culture, environment · Journalists killed

Minute 6

Genocide: the UN finding

The finding of the UN Commission of Inquiry

In September 2025 the UN Commission of Inquiry concluded that Israeli authorities and security forces in the Gaza Strip have committed and are continuing to commit genocide: it established four of the five acts of the Genocide Convention, and the intent to destroy was “the only reasonable inference” (A/HRC/60/CRP.3; A/80/337; follow-up report A/81/430, October 2026: continuing after the ceasefire). This is the finding of a body of the UN Human Rights Council, not a court judgment. → The finding in detail

Besides this Commission, the UN Special Rapporteur, Amnesty International, two Israeli human rights organisations and the International Association of Genocide Scholars have reached the same conclusion: genocide is being committed in Gaza. The International Court of Justice has not yet ruled – such cases take years – but in 2024 it saw a real risk to the Palestinians’ right to be protected from genocide. The duty of all states to prevent genocide applies now.

10bodies and organisations with a genocide finding
2024ICC arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant – suspicion, not a verdict

Read more (in German): The genocide question (English) · Prosecution · Warfare

Minute 7

West Bank: violence without punishment

Since October 2023 more than a thousand Palestinians have been killed in the West Bank, more than a fifth of them children. Settler attacks reached their highest level since UN records began in 2006 in October 2026; entire herding communities have been driven out. Thousands are held without charge in administrative detention. Prosecutions are rare: according to Yesh Din, fewer than 1 % of complaints against soldiers lead to an indictment.

1,143killed in the West Bank since Oct 2023, 250 of them children (UN)
3,101people in administrative detention without charge (Addameer, Oct 2026)
0.9 %of complaints against soldiers led to an indictment (Yesh Din)

Read more (in German): Settler violence · Detention & death penalty · Displacement in the West Bank

Minute 8

The Palestinians themselves: no elections, two authorities

Palestinians have not been able to vote since 2006. Since 2007 two authorities have ruled: Hamas in Gaza and the Fatah-led Palestinian Authority in the West Bank – both persecute and torture critics, and Hamas publicly executed people in 2025. The Palestinian Authority is broke because Israel withholds its tax revenues. 79 % of Palestinians want President Abbas to resign.

2006last parliamentary election
$3.67 bnPalestinian tax revenues withheld by Israel (UNCTAD)

Read more (in German): Palestinian politics · Economy under occupation · Sumud: resistance and criminalisation

Minute 9

The world: who sustains Israel and who objects

The United States gives Israel $3.8 billion in military aid every year and shields it with its veto in the Security Council; Germany is the second-largest arms supplier. The EU is Israel’s most important trading partner and does not enforce its own human rights clause. About 155 states recognise Palestine – Germany does not. At the same time dissent is growing, including among Jews in Israel and around the world.

$3.8 bnUS military aid per year
~155of 193 states recognise Palestine
52US vetoes in the Security Council since 1972

Read more (in German): USA and Israel · The EU and Israel · Recognition of Palestine · Jewish dissent · Neighbouring states

Minute 10

Germany

Germany supplies weapons, sided with Israel in the ICJ case and refuses to recognise Palestine. At home, demonstrations have been banned, events dissolved and people expelled – courts later declared several of these measures unlawful. At the same time hatred of Jews has risen sharply: police are recording record numbers of offences against Jews. The coverage of major media is the subject of studies and press council reprimands.

31 %of Israel’s major arms imports 2021–2025 came from Germany (SIPRI)
12press council reprimands on Middle East coverage since Oct 2023, 10 of them against Springer media
